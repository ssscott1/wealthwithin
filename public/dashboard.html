Module 3 – Section 3: Performance Metrics
Win/Loss Ratio = Winning trades ÷ Losing trades. A ratio > 1 means more wins than losses.
Profit/Loss Ratio = Average win ÷ Average loss. A ratio > 1 means average wins are larger than average losses.
Expectancy = (Win Rate × Avg Win) − (Loss Rate × Avg Loss). Positive expectancy confirms a profitable system over time.

WW Theory Guide
Quick reference for Diploma of Share Trading — Modules 1–3
MODULE 3Dow's Trend Theory

An uptrend is defined by a series of higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL). A downtrend by lower highs (LH) and lower lows (LL).

Entry Rules: Enter a long on confirmation of a new HH after a HL has been formed, ideally with increasing volume.
Exit Rules: Exit when price fails to make a new HH, or when it closes below the last HL (trend broken).
Volume Confirmation: Volume should increase on up-waves and decrease on down-waves (corrections) in an uptrend.
MODULE 3Gann's Swing Theory

Identifies swing highs and swing lows to determine trend direction and place stops.

Swing High: A bar whose high is higher than the bars on either side.
Swing Low: A bar whose low is lower than the bars on either side.
Entry: Enter long on confirmation of a swing low forming in an uptrend.
Stop Placement: Place stop-loss just below the last confirmed swing low.
MODULE 3Gann's Trend Theory
Trade in the direction of the main trend using a combination of swing highs/lows and Gann angles. The 1×1 angle (45°) represents a balanced market. Price above = bullish; below = bearish.
Entry: Buy when price bounces from a Gann angle support level in an uptrend with volume confirmation.
MODULE 3Gann's Counter Trend Theory
Higher-risk strategy trading against the main trend at key reversal zones. Requires strong volume confirmation and strict risk management.
Entry: Look for key reversal bars (outside days, key reversals) at major support/resistance in oversold/overbought zones.
Risk: Use tight stops. Counter-trend trades are lower probability — only take with exceptional setups.
MODULE 3Trend Line Theory
Uptrend line: Connect at least 2 swing lows. The more touches, the stronger the line.
Downtrend line: Connect at least 2 swing highs.
Entry: Buy on a bounce from an uptrend line support, ideally with a bullish bar pattern and volume spike.
Break: A close through the trend line is a warning signal. Confirmed break = exit or reverse.
MODULE 2Volume & Bar Analysis
Volume Principles: Volume should confirm the trend. High volume on rallies, low volume on corrections = healthy uptrend.
Volume Spike: Unusual volume (3×+ average) on a breakout confirms conviction.
Bar Patterns: Outside day (range exceeds prior bar), key reversal (new high then close below prior close), narrow range bars (NRB — potential explosive move ahead).
Bar Colour: Bullish = close above open (green/white). Bearish = close below open (red/black). Closing position within bar range is important.
MODULE 1Top-Down Analysis Framework
Step 1 — Market Analysis: Is the broader market (ASX 200) in an uptrend, downtrend, or sideways? Only trade longs in an uptrending market.
Step 2 — Sector Analysis: Identify the leading sectors using relative strength. Focus on sectors outperforming the index.
Step 3 — Stock Selection: Within the leading sector, identify the leading stocks. Apply Dow/Gann entry criteria to the best setups.
Note: Never fight the market trend. A great stock setup in a downtrending market has significantly lower probability.
MODULE 3 – SECTION 3Performance Metrics
Win Rate = Winning trades ÷ Total closed trades × 100
Win/Loss Ratio = Number of winning trades ÷ Number of losing trades
Profit/Loss Ratio = Average profit per winning trade ÷ Average loss per losing trade
Expectancy = (Win Rate × Avg Win) − (Loss Rate × Avg Loss) — must be positive for a profitable system
Example: 55% win rate, 2:1 P/L ratio → Expectancy = (0.55 × $2) − (0.45 × $1) = $0.65 per $1 risked
